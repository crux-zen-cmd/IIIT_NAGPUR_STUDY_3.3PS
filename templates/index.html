🔒 Privacy, Agency & Data Governance

Your cognitive telemetry belongs to you. Transparent on-device computation with zero raw video transfer.

💻

100% Local CV Inference

MediaPipe FaceMesh executes directly within your browser runtime using WebGL/WebAssembly. Landmark vectors are analyzed in volatile memory.

🚫

Zero Raw Video Stored

Camera frames are never uploaded, recorded, or saved to any disk or external server. Only numerical telemetry (EAR, head pose angles) is processed.

👁️

Zero Screen Capture

The study tracker does not record your screen or take screenshots. Page context is derived exclusively through URL platform tags and active window title metadata.

🛡️

Metadata-Only Event Logs

Interruption events track notification category (MESSAGE, EMAIL) and time duration. Message contents, keystrokes, and text are never inspected.
